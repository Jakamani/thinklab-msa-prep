# ThinkLab MSA Prep — Writing Part 2 (all three levels)

ThinkLab MSA Prep is a responsive, student-friendly website for Berlin MSA English practice. Exercises are original and modelled on examination formats, not copied from official exams.

## Available modules

- Reading Parts 1, 2 and 3 — three levels in each part.
- Writing Part 1 — three photo-writing levels, with original AI-generated photographs credited to ChatGPT (OpenAI).
- Writing Part 2 — all three levels:
  1. **Learn with clues — A Creative Afternoon at School?** Guided online discussion with four selectable ideas, an editable starter, self-check and model response.
  2. **Practise like the exam — A Later Start for School?** Independent online-discussion reply, four questions, minimum 100 words.
  3. **Take the challenge — AI and Homework: Help or Shortcut?** Independent online-discussion reply requiring balanced reasoning and a justified school rule, four questions, minimum 100 words.

## Marking and feedback

Writing Part 2 follows the 2026 Berlin MSA format: reply to an online discussion, address all four questions, and write **at least 100 words**. Its assessment criteria award 5 marks for content and 5 for language. After submission, students receive word-count feedback, task-specific self-checks and access to a model response; the site **does not award automated writing marks**. Students should share their work with their teacher for a score out of 10.

## Privacy and progress

There are no student logins, server-side submissions or analytics. Drafts, completion and results are stored locally in the browser. The original `thinklab-msa-prep-progress-v1` storage key remains unchanged, so existing progress normally carries over at the same website address. Students can export/import their progress.
